about Doodle Box

Meet your teacher, Sarah-Jayne

Doodle Box is run by Sarah-Jayne Reid, an artist, art therapist and educator. Her background is in Fine Art painting and Art Therapy, and she is a qualified and accredited member of the Irish Association of Creative Art Therapists.

Small classes mean individual attention. Children try two- and three-dimensional work with traditional and unusual materials, learn about famous and contemporary artists, and become more confident, independent and creative along the way.

Each year their work goes on show: Kildare students at Maynooth Library in December, and Dublin students at the RUA RED café in May.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions parents ask most. Can't see yours? Call +353 87 221 7924 or send us a message.

Where and when are classes?

At RUA RED, South Dublin Arts Centre in Tallaght (Thursdays and Saturdays), Newbridge Town Hall (Wednesdays) and Maynooth Community Space (Fridays). See the class list for each studio.

What ages do you cater for?

Doodle Bugs are ages 5 to 8, Doodle Buddies are 9 to 12, and Doodle Bots are our teen classes.

How long is each term?

Terms follow the school year and usually run for 11 or 12 weeks, with a week off for mid-term break.

What if my child misses a class?

Let us know in advance and we'll try to fit your child into another class for their age group that week.

Can siblings attend?

Yes, siblings are very welcome and there's a sibling discount. Just mention it when you book.

My child has special needs. Can you include them?

All children are welcome and we do our best to support every child. We can't provide an individual assistant, but parents have been able to bring one. Please get in touch to talk about your child's needs.
